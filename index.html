About Us

Ey-shaa Farms is a limited liability company that was registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission with its headquarters in Nasarawa State, Nigeria, and a regional office in Kaduna State.

Over the years, we have been working with communities where our farms are located to increase food sufficiency and foster agro-business growth. We are dedicated to collaborating with individuals, local, and international organizations to address food insecurity and ensure sustainability in Nigeria.
