Meet Our Management Team

Ahmed Saidu Ey-shaa - Director

Ahmed is a director in Ey-shaa Farms. He is an alumni of Bayero University Kano for his first degree, he obtained three different master's degrees from Ahmadu Bello University Zaria and his PhD at Federal University Lafia, Nasarawa state, Nigeria. He is a community and social development expert, a consultant, an administrator, and a resource person with many years of experience. He has developed himself over the years in agriculture, agribusiness, community development, and security.
