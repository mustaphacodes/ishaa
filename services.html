Our Services

At Ey-shaa Farms, we offer a range of services to support sustainable agriculture, investment, and agro-business growth.

Our Key Services

Leasing of Land

We offer leasing services for prospective farmers and investors looking to engage in agricultural activities. Our leased lands are strategically located for optimal agricultural use.

Linking Merchants with Foreign Investors

We facilitate connections between local merchants and foreign investors for export purposes, fostering international trade and business expansion.

Commodity and Storage

We provide storage facilities and commodity management services for agro-products, ensuring proper handling, storage, and distribution for maximum value retention.

Consultancy Services

We offer expert consultancy services for agricultural development and investment, helping businesses grow sustainably with informed strategies and planning.

Agricultural Training

We provide training programs for farmers and agricultural professionals, enhancing their skills and knowledge in modern farming techniques, sustainability, and innovation.

About Ey-shaa Farms

Ey-shaa Farms is dedicated to advancing agricultural practices through innovative solutions. Our commitment lies in sustainable farming techniques, empowering local farmers, and supporting agribusiness development. We aim to foster growth in the agricultural sector by providing expert services, training programs, and consultation, ensuring farmers thrive in today’s modern agricultural landscape.

We work closely with farmers to introduce technology-driven farming practices, ensuring they are well-equipped to succeed in an ever-changing world. Join us in our mission to transform the future of agriculture for the better!
