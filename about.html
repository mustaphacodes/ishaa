About Us

Ey-shaa Farms is a premier agricultural company committed to enhancing food security and sustainable farming practices. We operate from our headquarters in Nasarawa State, Nigeria, and maintain a regional office in Kaduna State. Our mission is to tackle food insecurity, improve agricultural practices, and empower local communities.

Founded in 2015, Ey-shaa Farms has grown to become a leading player in Nigeria’s agricultural sector, working with local farmers, government bodies, and international organizations to drive innovative solutions in farming. Our key areas of focus include crop production, livestock farming, and agro-processing. We are also heavily involved in training and development programs aimed at educating the next generation of farmers.

We pride ourselves on our sustainable practices, ensuring that our operations are environmentally friendly and economically viable. Through strategic partnerships and investments, Ey-shaa Farms is determined to create a positive impact on both local and international agricultural markets.
