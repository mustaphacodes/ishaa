Welcome to Ey-shaa Farms

About Us

Ey-shaa Farms is a limited liability company registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. Our headquarters is located in Nasarawa State, Nigeria, with a regional office in Kaduna State. We have been dedicated to working with local communities to increase food sufficiency and foster agro-business growth. We aim to collaborate with individuals, local, and international organizations to tackle food insecurity and ensure sustainability within Nigeria.

Our Services

Farm Management

We offer expert guidance and support for all aspects of farm operations, ensuring sustainability and growth.

Agricultural Training

Empowering farmers with the latest knowledge and skills, helping them thrive in the agricultural industry.

Agribusiness Consulting

We provide consulting services to develop sustainable and profitable agribusiness ventures, ensuring long-term success.
